# AI Security Review

AI can improve analyst speed while introducing prompt-injection, data-leakage and over-trust risks.

Required controls: treat model input as untrusted data; minimise sensitive information; restrict tools with least privilege; log model/tool activity; validate outputs against authoritative telemetry; require human approval before containment or account changes.

MITRE ATLAS: map each scenario to the applicable ATLAS technique during a real engagement. This portfolio lab keeps mappings conservative where synthetic examples do not justify a precise claim.

Operating model: AI proposes triage hypotheses and enrichment; the analyst verifies evidence, records the decision and remains accountable.
