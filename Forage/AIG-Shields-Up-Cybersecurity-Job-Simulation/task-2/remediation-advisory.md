# Task 3 — Remediation Advisory

## Objective

Translate vulnerability intelligence into concise guidance that an infrastructure-owning team can act on.

## Advisory Coverage

The drafted communication addressed:

- CVE-2021-44228 / Log4Shell.
- Remote-code-execution risk.
- Verification of installed Log4j versions.
- Patching affected components.
- Checking applications and bundled dependencies.
- Applying relevant mitigations where immediate patching was not possible.
- Reviewing logs for suspicious activity.
- Confirming remediation.

## Communication Principle

**What is affected → why it matters → what to verify → what to remediate → how to validate**

## Scope

The advisory was produced for the controlled Forage simulation and was not sent to a real AIG infrastructure team.
