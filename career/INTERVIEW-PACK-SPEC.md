# Job-Specific Interview Pack Specification

For every high-relevance vacancy, create one private interview pack tied to the canonical job ID.

## Required sections

1. Vacancy and employer context
2. Top requirements extracted from the vacancy
3. 10–20 likely technical questions
4. 10 behavioral questions
5. Role-specific questions
6. SOC/cybersecurity scenarios where applicable
7. STAR-formatted answer guidance
8. Employer-requirement questions
9. Technology-specific questions
10. Questions grounded in verified candidate experience
11. GitHub project questions
12. Forage questions, explicitly labeled as virtual job simulations
13. Likely screening questions
14. Practical/technical assessment preparation
15. Questions to ask the interviewer
16. Evidence-safe model answer guidance
17. Explicit claims/experience to avoid

## Evidence rules

Answers must use only verified candidate information. Do not invent employment, years of experience, certifications, clearance, visa/work authorization, metrics, technology/tool experience, security incidents handled, or employment status for simulations.

Forage simulations are virtual work experiences and must never be represented as employment.

## Trigger

Generate the pack when a vacancy reaches the configured high-relevance threshold and has a canonical job record. Keep the generated pack in the private career workspace.
