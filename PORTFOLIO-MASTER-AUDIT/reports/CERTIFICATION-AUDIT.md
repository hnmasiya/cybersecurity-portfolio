# Certification Naming Audit

Recommended standardized naming:

- CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)
- Google Cybersecurity Certificate
- Google IT Support Certificate
- National Diploma in Information Communication Technology
- BSc Computer Science — In Progress, if applicable

Avoid ambiguous wording such as:

- Google Professional
- Google Professional Cybersecurity
- Google Professional IT Support

unless the exact official credential name is being used.
